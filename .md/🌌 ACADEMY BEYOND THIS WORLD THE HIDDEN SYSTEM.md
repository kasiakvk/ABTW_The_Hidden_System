## 1. CORE VISION

### What Is ABTW: The Hidden System?
A **perception-driven 3D narrative adventure** adapted from the middle-grade cosmic mystery book series. Players step into the role of **Astra** — a girl who begins to notice things that should not exist: delayed shadows, shifting reflections, incomplete symbols, corridors that lead somewhere different each time.

This is **not** a game about combat, power, or spectacle.  
It is a game about **noticing, understanding, and deciding**.

### Core Design Pillars
| Pillar | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Perception Over Power** | The player grows by noticing more, not by becoming stronger |
| **Emotional Intelligence as Mechanic** | Patience, observation, and connection unlock deeper layers |
| **Cinematic Restraint** | 70% normal world, 30% strange. Anomalies are subtle, not spectacular |
| **Consequence Without Punishment** | Every decision shapes reality differently — no "wrong" path |
| **Living World** | The Academy is a character — emotionally intelligent and responsive |

### Core Game Loop
```
EXPLORE → NOTICE → INTERPRET → DECIDE → WORLD CHANGES → REFLECT → REPEAT
```

### Genre & Platform
| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Genre | Narrative 3D Perception Adventure |
| Sub-genre | Exploration / Light RPG / Mystery |
| Platform | PC (primary), Console (future) |
| Camera | 3rd person — cinematic, close follow |
| Target Audience | Middle-grade / YA (10–16), adults who love narrative games |
| Tone | Calm, curious, mysterious, emotionally warm |
| Comparable Titles | Journey, Inside, Spiritfarer, What Remains of Edith Finch |

## 2. STORY & NARRATIVE

### Series Context
ABTW: The Hidden System covers **Arc 1 — Discovery** (Books 1–4):

| Game Chapter | Book | Arc Theme |
|---|---|---|
| Chapter 1–4 | Book 1: The First Glitch | Learning how to see |
| Chapter 5–8 | Book 2: The Notebook That Writes | Recording reality |
| Chapter 9–12 | Book 3: The Hidden Symbols | Understanding signs |
| Chapter 13–16 | Book 4: The Corridor That Moves | Space is not stable |

### The Central Story
Astra is a new student at the Academy. From her first day, she notices things others do not: a shadow that moves a half-second too late, a reflection that shows different words, a corridor that leads somewhere it should not.

She meets **Lumi** (logic, analysis, notebook) and **Nela** (instinct, timing, alignment). Together they form a trio whose combined perception can unlock layers of the Academy no single student could reach alone.

> **Central question:** *"What does it mean that reality responds to how you see it?"*

### Chapter 1 Synopsis — "The First Glitch"
Astra arrives at the Academy. The East Corridor is her introduction — beautiful, calm, slightly too-perfect. She notices the first anomaly: her shadow moves a fraction of a second too late.

**First major decision:**
- **RECORD** the pattern → gain clarity, stabilise the world, close the deeper version
- **WAIT** → allow the pattern to evolve, unlock a deeper layer, enable Trio Activation

|--------|--------|
| Horror story | Cinematic stillness |
| Fantasy epic | Emotional warmth |
| School comedy | Layered mystery |
| Chosen one narrative | Perception-driven discovery |

## 3. CHARACTERS

### The Trio

#### ASTRA — The Perceiver (Player Character)
- **Role:** Primary playable character
- **Age:** 12–13
- **Core Trait:** Emotionally grounded, quietly observant, thoughtful before acting
- **Arc:** "Something is wrong" → "Reality responds to perception"
- **Stats Affinity:** Awareness, Timing
- **Visual DNA (CANONICAL):**
  - Hair: Dark indigo-purple, slightly wavy, medium length
  - Signature: Small star-shaped hair clip (left side)
  - Bracelet: Glows softly — reacts to anomaly proximity
  - Outfit: Oversized cosmic hoodie (deep navy/indigo), comfortable trousers, worn trainers
  - Palette: Deep navy, indigo, violet, soft gold accents

#### LUMI — The Analyst
- **Role:** AI companion / Player 2 (future multiplayer)
- **Core Trait:** Analytical, calm, notebook-centred, finds comfort in structure
- **Arc:** "Everything can be understood" → "Understanding also requires emotional openness"
- **Stats Affinity:** Clarity, Stability
- **Visual DNA:** Light blonde hair, crisp white shirt, soft mint cardigan, navy trousers
- **Signature:** Always holding or near her notebook

#### NELA — The Aligner
- **Role:** AI companion / Player 3 (future multiplayer)
- **Core Trait:** Energetic, improvisational, movement-oriented, feels before she thinks
- **Arc:** "Follow the feeling" → "Connection itself changes reality"
- **Stats Affinity:** Connection, Timing
- **Visual DNA:** Warm auburn curly hair, layered warm tones, mismatched earrings (star + moon)

### Supporting Characters
| Character | Role | Tone |
|---|---|---|
| Prof. Lyra Quill | Mentor — knows more than she reveals | Warm, measured, slightly melancholy |
| Archivist Noctra | Lorekeeper of the Archive | Ancient, quiet, speaks rarely |

- Characters **NEVER** change their visual design — growth is internal, not visual
- Expressions must be emotionally readable but **never exaggerated**
- Colour palettes are identity — mixing palettes in a single outfit is **forbidden**
- Silhouettes must be immediately distinguishable in shadow alone

## 4. WORLD & ENVIRONMENTS

### The Academy — Core Identity
The Academy is not a building. It is not a school. It is a **living perception system** — ancient, emotionally intelligent, and responsive to those who learn to notice it.

> *"The Academy does not reveal itself. It responds to perception."* — Golden World Rule

**The Academy IS:** alive, emotionally intelligent, layered, ancient but evolving, beautiful without spectacle  
**The Academy IS NOT:** a static fantasy school, a combat arena, a spectacle magic castle, a dark horror labyrinth

### Environment Emotional DNA
| Location | Emotional Function | Anomaly Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| East Corridor | Observation — the world is slightly wrong | High (tutorial) |
| Observatory | Wonder — scale and cosmic awareness | Medium (symbolic) |
| Archive / Library | Hidden truth — knowledge that is incomplete | Medium (layered) |
| Classrooms | Social warmth — belonging and routine | Low (contrast) |
| Dormitories | Vulnerability — safety and memory | Very low (safe space) |
| Gardens | Reflection — nature as emotional mirror | Low (seasonal) |
| Rooftops | Perspective — seeing the whole picture | High (activation) |
| Hidden Halls | Deep mystery — the system beneath the system | Very high (hidden layer) |

### Corridor States
| State | Visual | Lighting |
|---|---|---|
| NORMAL | Calm, warm, stable. Shadows correct. | Warm gold dominant |
| GLITCH | Shadows delayed 0.3–0.5s. Reflections slightly wrong. | Brief cool shift |
| ACTIVATION | Symbols forming. Light becomes directional. | Gold intensifies, violet undertone |

### Seasonal & Time Systems
| Time | Anomaly Behaviour | Emotional Tone |
|---|---|---|
| Morning | Low frequency, subtle | Quiet awareness |
| Afternoon | Medium, social | Warm rhythm |
| Sunset | High, transitional | Emotional shift |
| Night | Very high, perception depth | Silence, mystery |
| Rain | Unstable, unpredictable | Reflection, instability |

## 5. GAME MECHANICS & SYSTEMS

### Player Stats — The Perception System
| Stat | Symbol | Function |
|---|---|---|
| Awareness | 👁 | Detect anomalies |
| Clarity | 🧩 | Understand patterns |
| Connection | 🔗 | Link elements |
| Timing | ⏳ | Correct moment |
| Stability | 🌀 | Resist distortion |

### Academy Rank Progression
| Rank | Title | Unlock |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Observer | Basic anomaly detection |
| 2 | Noticer | Pattern recognition |
| 3 | Pattern Seeker | Symbol detection |
| 4 | Interpreter | Symbol meaning |
| 5 | Aligner | Trio Activation available |
| 6 | System Aware | Seasonal anomalies visible |
| 7 | Influence Initiate | Choices affect world state more |
| 8 | Reality Reader | Memory Walk unlocked |
| 9 | System Shifter | Hidden Layer fully accessible |
| 10 | Beyond Observer | Academy responds to presence alone |

### The Notebook System
> *"The Notebook is not a quest log. It is a reality-affecting system. What Astra records changes the world."*

**RECORD vs WAIT Decision:**
| Choice | Immediate Effect | Long-term Effect |
|---|---|---|
| RECORD | Pattern stabilises. +Clarity, +EXP. | Pattern ends. Echo appears later. |
| WAIT | Pattern evolves. Anomaly intensifies. | Deeper layers unlock. Trio possible. |
| DEEP RECORD (Trio) | Full pattern captured. Max reward. | World permanently changed. No Echo. |

### The Echo System
> *"The world does not reset. It echoes."*

If a player closes a path, the system creates an **Echo version** — weaker, incomplete, but present.
- Echo anomalies appear at ~40% of original strength
- Echo notebook entries are visually faded
- Echo symbols are always missing the most meaningful segment

### The Trio System
**Activation conditions:**
1. Astra observing an active anomaly (Awareness check)
2. Lumi has analysed the pattern (Clarity check)
3. Nela senses the correct moment (player must NOT click for 3 seconds)

**Visual sequence:** Environment dims → Lumi's notebook draws itself → Nela: "Wait… now." → Golden pulse → Hidden layer appears

### Symbol System — 7 States
`Hidden → Glitch → Forming → Activation → Responding → Deepening → Consequence`

## 6. VISUAL & ART DIRECTION

### World Colour DNA
| Colour | Hex | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Deep Navy | `#1A2942` | Mystery, perception, depth |
| Indigo | `#2D3A6B` | Cognitive depth, understanding |
| Violet | `#5B4A8A` | Instability, hidden layers |
| Warm Gold | `#C9A84C` | Reality anchor, activation |
| Muted Magenta | `#8B4A6B` | Awareness, perception shift |
| Ivory / Parchment | `#F9F2E7` | Knowledge, memory, safety |
| Soft Cyan | `#7EC8C8` | Analysis, clarity (Lumi) |

### Rendering Style
- **Overall:** Painterly softness — Studio Ghibli meets Journey
- **Characters:** Rounded forms, soft outlines, no harsh edges
- **Environments:** Slightly stylised — proportions grander than reality
- **Shaders:** Soft light, no specular highlights, subtle ambient occlusion
- **Anomaly FX:** Shader-based distortion only — NO particle explosions

### Forbidden Visual Directions
- ❌ Explosive or dramatic magical effects
- ❌ Combat-style visual language
- ❌ Grimdark or horror aesthetics
- ❌ Cyberpunk or sci-fi drift
- ❌ Spectacle fantasy (dragons, floating castles)
- ❌ Empty architecture showcases
- ❌ Game-level feeling
- ❌ Fantasy theme park aesthetics

## 7. CANON RULES & FORBIDDEN DRIFT

### The Five Golden Rules
1. *"The Academy does not reveal itself. It responds to perception."*
2. *"Magic is not shown. It is noticed."*
3. *"The stronger the anomaly becomes, the quieter the presentation should feel."*
4. *"Characters reveal the world through perception — not through power."*
5. *"Wonder > Power. Perception > Combat. Discovery > Domination."*

### Series Evolution Rules
- Characters **NEVER** change their visual design
- The Academy **NEVER** becomes fully explained
- The Hidden System **NEVER** behaves like a traditional magic system
- Every expansion must preserve: emotional atmosphere, perception-first storytelling, cinematic silence, world restraint

---

*ABTW succeeds when the player feels: "Reality itself is listening."*  
*NOT: "The world is showing off magic."*
