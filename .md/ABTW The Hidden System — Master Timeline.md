## 1. TIMELINE OVERVIEW

This document tracks all canonical events across the ABTW series — from the Academy's founding through all twelve books. It serves as the authoritative reference for narrative continuity, symbol emergence, and world-state changes.

### HOW TO READ THIS TIMELINE
- **[PRE]** = Pre-series events (before Astra arrives)
- **[B1]–[B12]** = Book number
- **[CH]** = Chapter within book
- **[HIDDEN]** = Events not directly witnessed by the Trio — inferred or discovered later
- **★** = Major canon event — cannot be changed or retconned

## 2. PRE-SERIES TIMELINE — THE ACADEMY BEFORE ASTRA

### [PRE-ANCIENT] — The Academy's Origin
- ★ The Academy is established — exact date unknown, intentionally
- ★ The Hidden System is embedded into the Academy's architecture
- ★ The first symbols are placed — their meaning is not recorded
- ★ The Academy begins responding to perception — the first documented response is lost

### [PRE-RECENT] — The Generation Before
- [HIDDEN] A previous student group discovers Layer 2 symbols
- [HIDDEN] They record too early — the first Echo generation is created
- [HIDDEN] The Memory Traces in the Dormitory Hall are left by this group
- [HIDDEN] Archivist Noctra witnesses this — and says nothing

### [PRE-ARRIVAL] — Before Book 1
- Lumi arrives at the Academy — begins filling her first notebook immediately
- Nela arrives — immediately senses something is different about the East Corridor
- Prof. Lyra Quill is assigned as their mentor — she already knows about the Hidden System
- Mr. Vale / The Curator begins his systematic documentation of "normal" Academy behaviour
- Orion Vex arrives — begins competing with Lumi for academic recognition

## 3. ARC 1 — DISCOVERY (Books 1–4)
### Theme: Learning How to See

---

### BOOK 1 — THE FIRST GLITCH

**[B1-CH1] — Astra Arrives**
- ★ Astra enters the Academy for the first time via the East Corridor
- ★ First anomaly: Astra's shadow moves 0.4 seconds after her
- Astra meets Lumi — Lumi has already filled 3 pages of observations
- Astra meets Nela — Nela says "Don't write it yet"
- World state: Autumn, Afternoon, Clear

**[B1-CH2] — The Pattern Emerges**
- ★ The shadow delay is confirmed as a pattern — not random
- Lumi identifies the 0.4-second interval as mathematically consistent
- Nela senses a "second layer" beneath the shadow anomaly
- Player decision point: RECORD or WAIT
- If RECORD: Shadow stabilises, Echo flagged for later
- If WAIT: Shadow evolves, Trio timer begins

**[B1-CH3] — First Trio Activation**
- ★ First Trio Activation in the East Corridor
- Lumi's notebook begins drawing on its own
- Nela says "Wait… now."
- ★ SYM_001 (The Delayed Shadow) fully activates
- Hidden layer briefly visible — a corridor that shouldn't exist
- World state changes: Awareness +1 for all three

**[B1-CH4] — The Echo**
- If player recorded early: Shadow Echo appears
- Notebook entry: "This is not the original pattern. A trace of something recorded too early."
- Astra looks at the corridor: "It's not random."
- ★ Chapter 1 closes: The Academy is confirmed as a perception system

---

### BOOK 2 — THE NOTEBOOK THAT WRITES

**[B2-CH1] — The Notebook Responds**
- ★ Lumi's notebook begins writing entries without her input
- First self-written entry: "PATTERN: REFLECTED WORD — Library Archive"
- Astra and Nela witness this — different reactions (analysis vs. instinct)
- World state: Autumn → Winter transition

**[B2-CH2] — The Library Investigation**
- ★ SYM_003 (The Reflected Word) discovered in Library Archive
- Mirrors throughout the Archive show different words than their originals
- Archivist Noctra is present — watches but does not intervene
- First encounter with Level 2 Archive

**[B2-CH3] — The Memory Trace**
- ★ SYM_005 (The Memory Trace) discovered in Dormitory Hall
- Night-only visibility confirmed
- The outline matches no current student — it is from the previous generation
- Lumi identifies the proportions as belonging to someone approximately 15 years ago

**[B2-CH4] — Recording Reality**
- ★ The Notebook System is fully understood by the Trio
- Lumi establishes the PATTERNS / SYMBOLS / CONNECTIONS / MEMORY tab system
- First complete notebook entry with all three character notes
- World state: Winter, Night

---

### BOOK 3 — THE HIDDEN SYMBOLS

**[B3-CH1] — The Garden Spiral**
- ★ SYM_006 (The Garden Spiral) discovered in Academy Gardens
- Spring arrival — new growth follows the spiral pattern
- Lumi: "The growth rate is mathematically precise. Not natural."
- Nela: "Touch the center. Gently."
- World state: Spring, Morning

**[B3-CH2] — The Convergence Point**
- ★ SYM_004 (The Convergence Point) discovered at the Junction
- Only visible when all three are present simultaneously
- Lumi: "Geometrically, this point shouldn't exist in this architecture."
- ★ First Layer 3 symbol activation
- Hidden corridor briefly visible — leads toward the Hidden Halls

**[B3-CH3] — The First Map**
- ★ The Trio creates the first "map of meaning" — connecting all discovered symbols
- Constellation discovered: The First Pattern (SYM_001 + SYM_008)
- Constellation discovered: The Memory Web (SYM_003 + SYM_005)
- Orion Vex observes the map — his reaction is ambiguous

**[B3-CH4] — Understanding Signs**
- ★ The symbol system is understood as a language, not a collection of anomalies
- Prof. Lyra Quill gives the first direct hint: "You're reading it correctly."
- World state: Spring → Summer transition

---

### BOOK 4 — THE CORRIDOR THAT MOVES

**[B4-CH1] — Space Instability**
- ★ The East Corridor leads to a different location than it should
- Perception affects navigation — confirmed for the first time
- Lumi maps the discrepancy: 3 metres of corridor that shouldn't exist
- World state: Summer, Sunset

**[B4-CH2] — The Observatory Star Map**
- ★ SYM_007 (The Observatory Star Map) discovered
- Stars shift position when observed directly — stable only in peripheral vision
- Lumi: "These stars don't exist in any astronomical catalogue."
- Nela: "They're not stars. They're positions."
- ★ Trio Activation in the Observatory — full Layer 3 access

**[B4-CH3] — The Hidden Hall**
- ★ First entry into a Hidden Hall
- The space is similar to normal corridors but proportionally wrong
- All surfaces hold Layer 3 symbols
- The Trio spends 3 minutes here before the space closes
- World state: Summer, Night

**[B4-CH4] — Arc 1 Closes**
- ★ The Trio understands: the Academy is a perception system, not a building
- Astra's arc closes: "Something is wrong" → "Reality responds to perception"
- Lumi's arc progresses: "Everything can be understood" → beginning to question this
- Nela's arc progresses: "Follow the feeling" → "Connection itself changes reality"
- ★ Arc 1 complete: The Trio can now see all three layers

## 4. ARC 2 — UNDERSTANDING (Books 5–8)
### Theme: Learning How Reality Works

---

### BOOK 5 — THE MISSING CONNECTIONS

**Key Events:**
- ★ Emotional patterns between students become visible as anomalies
- Relationships affect the Hidden System — confirmed
- The Trio discovers that their own emotional states change what they can see
- First encounter with a "broken" connection — a symbol that won't activate

**World State Changes:**
- Autumn, Year 2
- New symbols emerge: emotional connection symbols (Layer 2)
- The Academy responds to the Trio's growing understanding

---

### BOOK 6 — THE BROKEN PATTERN

**Key Events:**
- ★ Something disrupts the symbol system — symbols stop behaving correctly
- The Trio must reinterpret everything they thought they understood
- Orion Vex is revealed to have been observing the system independently
- First encounter with a symbol that actively resists activation

**World State Changes:**
- Winter, Year 2
- Symbol instability increases
- Mr. Vale begins documenting "irregularities"

---

### BOOK 7 — THE SILENT ZONES

**Key Events:**
- ★ Areas discovered where nothing reacts — no symbols, no anomalies, no response
- The absence of meaning is itself meaningful
- The Trio's strongest emotional challenge — what does it mean when the system goes silent?
- Archivist Noctra speaks for the first time about the previous generation

**World State Changes:**
- Spring, Year 2
- Silent zones mapped — they form a pattern
- The pattern of silence is itself a symbol

---

### BOOK 8 — THE OBSERVER EFFECT

**Key Events:**
- ★ Astra's perception begins influencing outcomes — not just observing them
- The line between observer and creator begins to blur
- The Trio must decide: are they reading the system, or writing it?
- ★ Arc 2 complete: The Trio understands that reality responds to how it is seen

**World State Changes:**
- Summer, Year 2
- The Academy's response to the Trio becomes more direct
- Prof. Lyra Quill reveals she has been watching this development
