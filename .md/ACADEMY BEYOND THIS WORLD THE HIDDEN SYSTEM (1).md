## 1. GAME OVERVIEW & CORE VISION

### 1.1 What Is ABTW: The Hidden System?

Academy Beyond This World: The Hidden System is a perception-driven 3D narrative adventure game adapted from the middle-grade cosmic mystery book series of the same name. Players step into the role of Astra — a girl who begins to notice things that should not exist: delayed shadows, shifting reflections, incomplete symbols, and corridors that lead somewhere different each time.

This is not a game about combat, power, or spectacle. It is a game about noticing, understanding, and deciding. The world of the Academy responds not to force, but to perception — and every choice the player makes changes the version of reality they experience.

> *"This is not a game about winning. This is a game about seeing differently."*

### 1.2 Core Design Pillars

Every visual, mechanical, and narrative decision in ABTW must serve these five pillars:

- **Perception Over Power:** The player grows by noticing more, not by becoming stronger in a traditional sense.
- **Emotional Intelligence as Mechanic:** Patience, observation, and connection unlock deeper layers of the world.
- **Cinematic Restraint:** The world is 70% normal, 30% strange. Anomalies are subtle, not spectacular.
- **Consequence Without Punishment:** Every decision shapes reality differently. There is no "wrong" path — only different versions of the world.
- **Living World:** The Academy is not a backdrop. It is a character — emotionally intelligent, perceptive, and responsive.

### 1.3 Genre & Platform

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Genre | Narrative 3D Perception Adventure |
| Sub-genre | Exploration / Light RPG / Mystery |
| Platform | PC (primary), Console (future) |
| Camera | 3rd person — cinematic, close follow |
| Target Audience | Middle-grade / YA (10–16), adults who love narrative games |
| Tone | Calm, curious, mysterious, emotionally warm |
| Comparable Titles | Journey, Inside, Spiritfarer, What Remains of Edith Finch |

### 1.4 Core Game Loop

The fundamental rhythm of every session in ABTW follows this sequence:

**EXPLORE → NOTICE → INTERPRET → DECIDE → WORLD CHANGES → REFLECT → REPEAT**

This loop is the heartbeat of the game. Every environment, every anomaly, every UI element must support and reinforce this rhythm. Graphic artists should design with this loop in mind — each space should invite exploration, reward careful observation, and feel meaningfully different after a player decision.

## 2. STORY & NARRATIVE FOUNDATION

### 2.1 Series Context

ABTW: The Hidden System is the game adaptation of Book 1 — "The First Glitch." The game covers the events of Arc 1 (Discovery), spanning Books 1–4 of the twelve-book series.

| Game Chapter | Book | Arc Theme |
|---|---|---|
| Chapter 1–4 | Book 1: The First Glitch | Learning how to see |
| Chapter 5–8 | Book 2: The Notebook That Writes | Recording reality |
| Chapter 9–12 | Book 3: The Hidden Symbols | Understanding signs |
| Chapter 13–16 | Book 4: The Corridor That Moves | Space is not stable |

### 2.2 The Central Story

Astra is a new student at the Academy — an ancient institution that is far more than a school. From her first day, she notices things that others do not: a shadow that moves a half-second too late, a reflection that shows different words, a corridor that leads somewhere it should not.

She meets Lumi, who approaches every anomaly with logic and her ever-present notebook, and Nela, who feels the right moment to act before she can explain why. Together, the three form a trio whose combined perception, analysis, and alignment can unlock layers of the Academy that no single student could reach alone.

The central question of the game is not "What is the Academy hiding?" but rather: **"What does it mean that reality responds to how you see it?"**

### 2.3 Narrative Tone for Visual Production

The story must feel like a quiet mystery unfolding in real time. Graphic artists should understand the following tonal anchors:

- **NOT a horror story:** Anomalies are unsettling but never threatening. The mood is wonder, not fear.
- **NOT a fantasy epic:** There is no chosen one, no dark lord, no magical battle. The conflict is perceptual.
- **NOT a school comedy:** The Academy is warm and socially alive, but the tone is always slightly more serious than ordinary school life.
- **YES to cinematic stillness:** Long pauses, quiet corridors, the sound of footsteps echoing slightly wrong.
- **YES to emotional warmth:** Friendship, belonging, the comfort of a shared notebook, the safety of a dormitory room.
- **YES to layered mystery:** Every environment should feel like it is holding something back — not threatening, but aware.

### 2.4 Chapter 1 Synopsis — "The First Glitch"

Astra arrives at the Academy for the first time. The East Corridor is her introduction to the world — a beautiful, calm, slightly too-perfect space. As she walks, she notices the first anomaly: her shadow moves a fraction of a second too late.

She meets Lumi, who has already filled three pages of her notebook with observations. She meets Nela, who tells her to wait before writing anything down. The three discover that the shadow anomaly is not random — it is a pattern. And patterns, in the Academy, mean something.

The player's first major decision: record the pattern now (gaining clarity, stabilising the world) or wait (allowing the pattern to evolve, potentially unlocking a deeper layer). This decision shapes the rest of Chapter 1 and echoes through the entire game.

### 3.3 Character Rendering Rules

## 4. WORLD & ENVIRONMENT DESIGN

### 4.1 The Academy — Core Identity

The Academy is not a building. It is not a school. It is not a magical institution in the traditional sense. It is a **living perception system** — ancient, emotionally intelligent, and responsive to those who learn to notice it.

> *"The Academy does not reveal itself. It responds to perception."* — This principle governs every environment in the game.

**The Academy IS:**

- Alive — it breathes, shifts, and responds
- Emotionally intelligent — it feels the emotional state of those within it
- Layered — there are always more layers beneath what is visible
- Ancient but evolving — centuries old, but never static
- Beautiful without spectacle — cinematic, not theatrical

**The Academy is NOT:**

- A static fantasy school — it is never just a backdrop
- A combat arena — there are no battle spaces
- A spectacle magic castle — no floating towers, no dramatic magical displays
- A dark horror labyrinth — unsettling, yes; threatening, never
- A dystopian institution — the Academy is fundamentally safe and warm

### 4.2 Environment Emotional DNA

Every environment in ABTW has a specific emotional function. Graphic artists must understand not just what a space looks like, but what it is supposed to make the player feel:

| Location | Emotional Function | Pacing Role | Anomaly Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| East Corridor | Observation — the world is slightly wrong | Introduction / tension build | High (tutorial space) |
| Observatory | Wonder — scale and cosmic awareness | Breathing / reflection | Medium (symbolic) |
| Archive / Library | Hidden truth — knowledge that is incomplete | Investigation / slow burn | Medium (layered) |
| Classrooms | Social warmth — belonging and routine | Grounding / normalcy | Low (contrast) |
| Dormitories | Vulnerability — safety and memory | Emotional recovery | Very low (safe space) |
| Gardens | Reflection — nature as emotional mirror | Breathing / reset | Low (seasonal) |
| Rooftops | Perspective — seeing the whole picture | Climactic / revelatory | High (activation) |
| Hidden Halls | Deep mystery — the system beneath the system | Climax / discovery | Very high (hidden layer) |

### 4.3 Corridor System — Level 1 Environment

The East Corridor is the primary environment for Chapter 1 and the most important space for graphic production to get right. It is the player's first experience of the Academy and must establish the visual language of the entire game.

**East Corridor — Visual Specification:**

- Architecture: High vaulted ceilings, stone with warm wood accents, large windows on one side
- Lighting: Warm afternoon gold from windows, cool ambient from ceiling — the contrast creates depth
- Colour temperature: Warm (3200K equivalent) from natural light, cool (5500K) from ambient — this contrast is intentional
- Floor: Dark polished stone that reflects light imperfectly — reflections are slightly wrong even in normal state
- Scale: Corridors must feel larger than the characters — this is a design rule, not a suggestion
- Texture: Aged but maintained — not crumbling, not pristine; lived-in and ancient

**Corridor States — Visual Guide:**

| State | Visual Characteristics | Lighting Change | Sound Design Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| NORMAL | Calm, warm, stable. Shadows behave correctly. Reflections are accurate. | Warm gold dominant | Ambient hum, distant footsteps |
| GLITCH | Shadows delayed 0.3–0.5 seconds. Reflections show slightly different content. Light flickers once, then stabilises. | Brief cool shift, returns to warm | Low hum, slight echo distortion |
| ACTIVATION | Responsive. Layered. Symbols begin to form on surfaces. Light becomes directional — pointing toward the pattern. | Gold intensifies, violet undertone appears | Silence, then soft pulse |

### 4.4 Observatory System

The Observatory is the second major environment and represents the game's emotional peak in Chapter 1. It is where the player first understands that the Academy's anomalies are not random — they are a system.

- Atmosphere: Cool navy, gold observational light, layered silence, distant stars
- Ceiling: Star map that does not match any known constellation — stars shift position when observed directly
- Telescope: Central prop — ancient, brass, slightly too large for the room
- Floor: Circular, with an incomplete circle symbol permanently etched — the gap rotates between visits
- Emotional target: Quiet wonder, cosmic scale, the feeling of being very small in a very large system

### 4.5 Archive / Library System

The Archive is the Academy's memory — a space where past events leave visible traces and knowledge is always incomplete.

- Architecture: Underground levels, high shelves, narrow passages between stacks
- Lighting: Warm amber reading lamps, cool blue from deeper sections — the deeper you go, the cooler the light
- Books: Some titles are readable, some are in unknown scripts, some change title between visits
- Mirrors: Placed throughout — reflections show different words than the originals they reflect
- Emotional target: Intelligent, quiet, ancient, emotionally weighted, partially hidden

### 4.6 Seasonal & Time Systems

The Academy changes with time and season. These changes are not decorative — they are mechanical, affecting anomaly frequency, emotional tone, and player stats.

| Time of Day | Visual Mood | Anomaly Behaviour | Emotional Tone |
|---|---|---|---|
| Morning | Soft gold, mist in gardens, long shadows | Low frequency, subtle | Quiet awareness, fresh start |
| Afternoon | Warm social light, busy corridors | Medium frequency, social | Warm rhythm, belonging |
| Sunset | Deep orange-gold, long dramatic shadows | High frequency, transitional | Emotional shift, anticipation |
| Night | Deep navy, silver moonlight, stars visible indoors | Very high, perception depth | Silence, mystery, depth |
| Rain | Grey-blue, reflective surfaces everywhere | Unstable, unpredictable | Reflection, instability |

## 5. GAME MECHANICS & SYSTEMS

### 5.1 Player Stats — The Perception System

Astra does not gain traditional RPG stats. She develops perceptual abilities — ways of seeing and understanding the world more deeply. These stats are invisible to the player in the traditional sense; they manifest as changes in what the player can see and interact with.

| Stat | Symbol | Function | Visual Manifestation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Awareness | 👁 | Detect anomalies — the more Awareness, the earlier glitches become visible | Subtle shimmer around anomaly locations |
| Clarity | 🧩 | Understand patterns — required to interpret what anomalies mean | Notebook entries become more detailed automatically |
| Connection | 🔗 | Link elements — required for Trio Activation | Thin golden threads appear between related anomalies |
| Timing | ⏳ | Correct moment — determines when Trio Activation triggers | Subtle pulse in the environment when timing is right |
| Stability | 🌀 | Resist distortion — prevents anomalies from overwhelming perception | Environment feels calmer, anomalies less intense |

### 5.2 Academy Rank Progression

As Astra's perception deepens, she advances through Academy Ranks. These are not power levels — they are recognition of how much of the world she can now see.

| Rank | Title | Unlock |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Observer | Basic anomaly detection — Layer 0 visible |
| 2 | Noticer | Pattern recognition — Layer 1 visible |
| 3 | Pattern Seeker | Symbol detection — Layer 2 begins to form |
| 4 | Interpreter | Symbol meaning — Layer 2 fully visible |
| 5 | Aligner | Trio Activation available — Layer 3 accessible |
| 6 | System Aware | World state tracking — seasonal anomalies visible |
| 7 | Influence Initiate | Player choices affect world state more significantly |
| 8 | Reality Reader | Memory Walk system unlocked |
| 9 | System Shifter | Hidden Layer fully accessible |
| 10 | Beyond Observer | Full perception — the Academy responds to presence alone |

### 5.3 The Notebook System — Core Mechanic

> *The Notebook is not a quest log. It is not a UI tool. It is a reality-affecting system. What Astra records changes the world.*

The Notebook is the most important mechanic in the game and the most important UI element for graphic production. It must feel like a real object — alive, slightly too full, and meaningful.

**Notebook Visual Specification:**

- Physical appearance: Worn leather cover, dark navy-indigo, slightly too thick for its binding
- Pages: Cream-coloured, slightly textured — not white, not yellow, but warm ivory
- Ink: Dark blue-black for Lumi's entries, softer violet for Astra's observations, warm amber for Nela's notes
- Tabs: Colour-coded by section — Patterns (navy), Symbols (violet), Connections (gold), Memory (silver)
- Animation: Pages turn with slight resistance; the notebook breathes subtly when an anomaly is nearby
- Special state: During Trio Activation, the notebook draws on its own — this is a key visual moment

**Notebook UI Layout:**

The Notebook UI must feel like opening a real notebook, not opening a game menu:

- Header: "ASTRA'S NOTEBOOK" in handwritten-style font
- Tabs: PATTERNS | SYMBOLS | CONNECTIONS | MEMORY — horizontal, colour-coded
- Pattern Entry format: Pattern name, Status indicator (Unrecorded / Recorded / Echo), Observations list, Character notes (Lumi / Nela), Action buttons
- Action buttons: `[ RECORD ]` and `[ WAIT ]` — these are the two most important UI elements in the game
- Echo entries: Visually faded, slightly distorted text, marked with a different ink colour

**The Record / Wait Decision:**

| Choice | Immediate Effect | Long-term Effect | Visual Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| RECORD | Pattern stabilises. World calms. +Clarity, +EXP. | Pattern ends. Deeper version locked. Echo appears later. | Shadows return to normal. Notebook entry fills in completely. |
| WAIT | Pattern evolves. Anomaly intensifies. Trio timer begins. | Deeper layers unlock. Hidden content accessible. Trio Activation possible. | Shadows spread. New symbols form. Environment responds. |
| DEEP RECORD (Trio) | Full pattern captured. Hidden layer recorded. Maximum reward. | Complete understanding. No Echo. World permanently changed. | Golden light fills the space. Symbol fully forms. Notebook glows briefly. |

### 5.4 The Anomaly System

Anomalies are the primary interactive elements of the game world. They are the Academy's way of communicating with those who can perceive it. For graphic artists, anomalies are the most technically demanding visual elements — they must be subtle enough to feel real, but distinct enough to be noticed.

**Anomaly Visual Rules:**

- 70% of the environment must look completely normal at all times
- Anomalies are never flashy — no particle explosions, no dramatic light shows, no combat effects
- The strongest anomaly should feel like something is slightly wrong, not like magic is happening
- Anomalies intensify gradually — they never appear at full strength immediately

**Anomaly Catalogue — Chapter 1:**

| Anomaly | Visual Description | Intensity | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shadow Delay | Shadow moves 0.3–0.5 seconds after its source. Colour: slightly cooler than normal shadow. | Low → Medium | East Corridor |
| Reflected Word | Text in reflective surfaces shows different words than the original. Ink colour: soft violet. | Low | Library Archive |
| Incomplete Circle | Circle symbol on wall — always missing one segment. Gap rotates between visits. | Medium | Observatory |
| Echo Corridor | Footsteps echo with slight delay and different rhythm. Visual: faint second shadow of footstep. | Low | West Corridor |
| Memory Trace | Faint outline of a figure that no longer stands there. Visible only at night. | Medium | Dormitory Hall |
| Garden Spiral | Plant growth follows a mathematically precise spiral. Expands by one rotation each season. | Low | Academy Gardens |

### 5.5 Symbol System

Symbols are the Academy's language — ancient, incomplete, and responsive. They are not decorative elements. They are interactive objects that evolve through seven states.

| State | Visual Appearance | Player Interaction |
|---|---|---|
| Hidden | Invisible — not present in the environment | None — must be unlocked by Awareness |
| Glitch | Barely visible — a shimmer, a wrong shadow, a reflection anomaly | Awareness check — player must notice |
| Forming | Partially visible — incomplete lines, fading in and out | Clarity check — player must understand |
| Activation | Fully visible — complete symbol, glowing softly in gold | Trio required — all three must be present |
| Responding | Animated — symbol pulses, environment reacts around it | Observation — player watches the response |
| Deepening | Symbol connects to others — golden threads appear | Connection — player links related symbols |
| Consequence | Symbol permanently marked in notebook — world changed | Record — the change is locked in |

### 5.6 The Echo System

> *The world does not reset. It echoes. If a player closes a path, the system creates an Echo version — weaker, incomplete, but present.*

The Echo System ensures that no player ever feels they have "broken" the game or permanently lost content. Every closed path leaves a trace — an Echo — that hints at what was there.

- Echo anomalies appear at reduced intensity — approximately 40% of original strength
- Echo notebook entries are visually faded — lighter ink, slightly blurred text
- Echo symbols are incomplete — the missing segment is always the most meaningful part
- Echo entries in the notebook read: *"This is not the original pattern. A trace of something recorded too early."*

### 5.7 The Trio System

The Trio System is the game's most powerful mechanic and its most important visual event. When Astra, Lumi, and Nela are all present, observing, and synchronised, the Academy responds in ways that no single student could achieve.

**Trio Activation Conditions:**

- Astra must be observing an active anomaly (Awareness check passed)
- Lumi must have analysed the pattern (Clarity check passed)
- Nela must sense the correct moment (Timing check passed — player must not click for 3 seconds)

**Trio Activation Visual Sequence:**

1. Environment subtly dims — not dark, but focused
2. Lumi's notebook begins drawing on its own — pages turn, symbols appear
3. Nela says "Wait… now." — the moment of alignment
4. A soft golden pulse radiates from the anomaly location
5. The hidden layer appears — new symbols, new paths, new understanding
6. UI shows "TRIO SYNCHRONIZATION" with +Connection, +Awareness indicators

**Forbidden:** Dramatic explosions, bright flashes, combat-style effects, dramatic music swells

## 6. VISUAL & ART DIRECTION

### 6.1 Core Visual Philosophy

> *"ABTW succeeds when the player feels: Reality itself is listening. NOT: The world is showing off magic."*

The visual language of ABTW is built on restraint, atmosphere, and emotional intelligence. Every visual decision must serve the story and the mechanics — never decoration for its own sake.

### 6.2 World Colour DNA

The Academy's colour system is not arbitrary. Each colour carries specific emotional and mechanical meaning. Graphic artists must understand and apply this system consistently across all environments, characters, and UI elements.

| Colour | Hex | Meaning | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Deep Navy | `#1A2942` | Mystery, perception, depth | Primary environment colour, night states, Lumi's palette |
| Indigo | `#2D3A6B` | Cognitive depth, understanding | Secondary environment, symbol states, Astra's palette |
| Violet | `#5B4A8A` | Instability, hidden layers | Anomaly states, glitch effects, symbol forming |
| Warm Gold | `#C9A84C` | Reality anchor, belonging, activation | Trio activation, notebook highlights, warm spaces |
| Muted Magenta | `#8B4A6B` | Awareness, perception shift | Perception Mode UI, awareness indicators |
| Slate Blue | `#4A5568` | Calm, grounded reality | Normal state environments, character outlines |
| Ivory / Parchment | `#F9F2E7` | Knowledge, memory, safety | Notebook pages, dormitory spaces, Archive lighting |
| Soft Cyan | `#7EC8C8` | Analysis, clarity | Lumi's notebook ink, Clarity stat indicators |

### 6.3 Lighting Philosophy

Lighting in ABTW is not just atmospheric — it is narrative. The quality and colour of light tells the player what kind of space they are in and what kind of perception is required.

- **Warm Gold (3200K):** Reality anchor. Spaces lit primarily in warm gold feel safe, grounded, and socially alive. Classrooms, dormitories, common areas.
- **Cool Navy (5500K+):** Cognitive depth. Spaces lit in cool blue-navy feel analytical, mysterious, and perceptually demanding. Observatories, archives, hidden halls.
- **Violet Undertone:** Instability. When violet appears in the lighting, something is wrong — or something is about to be revealed. Anomaly states, glitch moments.
- **Mixed (Gold + Navy):** Transition. The most interesting spaces — corridors, gardens at dusk — mix warm and cool light. This is where anomalies are most likely to appear.

**Lighting Rules:**

- Never use harsh directional light — all light sources should feel soft and diffused
- Shadows are always slightly softer than realistic — this is a stylistic choice, not a technical limitation
- Anomaly lighting changes must be subtle — a 10% shift in colour temperature, not a dramatic colour change
- The strongest lighting effect in the game is the Trio Activation gold pulse — and even this should feel like a breath, not an explosion

### 6.4 Rendering Style

ABTW uses a stylised cinematic rendering approach — not photorealistic, not cartoon, but something between: painterly, soft, emotionally resonant.

| Element | Style Specification |
|---|---|
| Overall style | Painterly softness — anime-inspired but not anime. Think Studio Ghibli meets Journey. |
| Character rendering | Rounded forms, soft outlines, no harsh edges. Skin tones warm and varied. |
| Environment rendering | Slightly stylised — architecture is real but proportions are slightly grander than reality. |
| Shader approach | Soft light shader — no specular highlights, no sharp shadows, subtle ambient occlusion only. |
| Texture approach | Hand-painted feel — textures should look like they were painted, not photographed. |
| Post-processing | Subtle colour grading, very light bloom on gold elements only, no lens flare, no chromatic aberration. |
| Anomaly FX | Shader-based — no particle systems for primary anomalies. Distortion, delay, colour shift only. |

### 6.5 Page Architecture — Illustration Types

For graphic production of the book-game hybrid materials, the following page types are defined and locked:

| Page Type | Description | Text/Illustration Ratio |
|---|---|---|
| Classic Split | Text left, illustration right. Standard narrative page. | 50% / 50% |
| Soft Hybrid | Illustration bleeds into text area with soft edges. Atmospheric. | 40% / 60% |
| Cinematic Full | Full-page illustration. No text except caption. Used for key moments. | 5% / 95% |
| Notebook / Observation | Styled as Lumi's notebook. Handwritten text, sketched illustrations. | 60% / 40% |
| Archive / Lore | Aged parchment feel. Dense text with marginal illustrations. | 70% / 30% |
| Reflection / Breathing | Minimal text, large atmospheric illustration. Pacing page. | 20% / 80% |

### 6.6 Forbidden Visual Directions

These visual approaches are explicitly forbidden in all ABTW production materials:

- Explosive or dramatic magical effects — no fireballs, no energy blasts, no dramatic power displays
- Combat-style visual language — no impact frames, no speed lines, no battle poses
- Grimdark or horror aesthetics — no blood, no decay, no threatening darkness
- Cyberpunk or sci-fi drift — the Academy is ancient and organic, never technological
- Spectacle fantasy — no dragons, no floating castles, no dramatic magical transformations
- Empty architecture showcases — every space must feel inhabited and emotionally alive
- Game-level feeling — environments must never look like game levels; they must look like real places
- Fantasy theme park aesthetics — the Academy is not Hogwarts; it is quieter, stranger, more real

## 7. GRAPHIC PRODUCTION GUIDELINES

### 7.1 Production Philosophy

> *"Atmosphere first. Prose first. Cinematic. Emotionally intelligent. Perception-driven. Restrained."* — ABTW Production Bible

The production hierarchy for all ABTW graphic materials is fixed. Every decision must be made in this order of priority:

**1. Readability → 2. Emotional Atmosphere → 3. Cinematic Pacing → 4. Environmental Storytelling → 5. Character Perception → 6. Illustration Quality → 7. Ornament → 8. FX**

### 7.2 Reader / Player Experience Target

Every piece of graphic production must be evaluated against this target experience. Ask: does this image make the viewer feel:

| Target Feeling | Achieved By |
|---|---|
| Immersed | Environments that feel real and inhabited, not staged |
| Emotionally calm | Soft lighting, warm colours, no aggressive visual elements |
| Curious | Details that reward close observation — things slightly wrong, slightly hidden |
| Observant | Compositions that guide the eye without forcing it |
| Connected | Characters who feel emotionally present, not posed |
| Uncertain (safely) | Anomalies that create productive unease, not fear |

### 7.4 QA Checklist — Per Illustration

Every illustration must pass these eight quality checks before delivery:

- ✓ **Emotional atmosphere check:** Does the image feel emotionally correct for its context?
- ✓ **Colour DNA check:** Are all colours within the approved palette? No unauthorised colour introductions.
- ✓ **Character consistency check:** Do all characters match their canonical visual DNA exactly?
- ✓ **Anomaly subtlety check:** Are anomalies subtle enough? (If you notice them immediately, they are too strong.)
- ✓ **Scale check:** Do environments feel larger than the characters?
- ✓ **Lighting check:** Is the lighting emotionally appropriate for the space and time of day?
- ✓ **Forbidden elements check:** No combat poses, no dramatic FX, no horror elements, no cyberpunk drift.
- ✓ **Readability check:** Can the viewer understand what is happening without text explanation?

### 7.5 Regeneration Criteria

An illustration must be regenerated (not revised) if it fails any of the following:

- Characters are visually inconsistent with their canonical design
- The emotional atmosphere is wrong — the image feels threatening, comedic, or generic
- Anomalies are too obvious or too dramatic — they break the 70/30 rule
- The colour palette drifts outside the approved World Colour DNA
- The image contains any forbidden visual element (combat, horror, spectacle FX)
- The composition does not serve the narrative function of the page type
- The lighting contradicts the emotional function of the space
- The image feels like a game level rather than a real place

## 9. CANON RULES & FORBIDDEN DRIFT

### 9.1 The Golden Rules

These rules govern all production decisions across all media — game, book, promotional, and transmedia:

> **Rule 1:** *"The Academy does not reveal itself. It responds to perception."*

> **Rule 2:** *"Magic is not shown. It is noticed."*

> **Rule 3:** *"The stronger the anomaly becomes, the quieter the presentation should feel."*

> **Rule 4:** *"Characters reveal the world through perception — not through power."*

> **Rule 5:** *"Wonder > Power. Perception > Combat. Discovery > Domination. Emotional Intelligence > Spectacle."*

### 9.2 Forbidden World Drift

These are the directions the ABTW world must never move in, regardless of creative pressure, market trends, or production convenience:

| Forbidden Direction | Why It Is Forbidden | Warning Signs |
|---|---|---|
| Dystopian collapse | The Academy is fundamentally safe and warm — it is a place of discovery, not danger | Dark corridors, threatening NPCs, survival mechanics |
| Grimdark horror | Anomalies create productive unease, not fear — the world is strange, not hostile | Jump scares, threatening darkness, body horror |
| Overdesigned magic systems | The Hidden System is incomplete and responsive — it never fully explains itself | Spell trees, magic schools, power levels |
| Cyberpunk drift | The Academy is ancient and organic — technology is not part of its identity | Neon lights, digital interfaces, futuristic elements |
| Spectacle fantasy | The world is 70% normal — spectacle breaks the perceptual contract with the player | Dragons, floating islands, dramatic magical battles |
| Empty architecture | Every space must feel inhabited and emotionally alive | Beautiful but lifeless environments, no social life |
| Game-level feeling | The Academy must feel like a real place, not a designed game space | Obvious level boundaries, game-y layouts, checkpoint markers |
| Fantasy theme park | The Academy is quieter and stranger than Hogwarts — it does not perform for visitors | Whimsical excess, obvious magical displays, tourist-friendly design |

### 9.3 Series Evolution Rules

These rules govern how the ABTW world evolves across books and game chapters:

- Characters **NEVER** change their visual design — growth is internal, not visual
- The Academy **NEVER** becomes fully explained — mystery is preserved across all twelve books
- The Hidden System **NEVER** behaves like a traditional magic system — it remains incomplete and responsive
- Every expansion must preserve: emotional atmosphere, perception-first storytelling, cinematic silence, world restraint
- New locations must feel like they belong to the same world — not like new biomes in a game
- New characters must feel emotionally believable — not like archetypes or NPCs

### 9.4 Final Canon Statement

This document is the highest-level production authority for Academy Beyond This World: The Hidden System. All graphic production, character design, environment art, UI design, and visual effects must conform to the rules, specifications, and guidelines contained herein.

Any deviation from the canonical visual DNA, character design, colour system, or world rules requires written approval from the creative director before production begins.
