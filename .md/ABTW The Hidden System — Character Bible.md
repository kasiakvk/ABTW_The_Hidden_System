## 1. THE TRIO — CORE CHARACTER SYSTEM

The three main characters are not interchangeable. Each represents a distinct mode of perception. Together they form a complete system. Separately, they can only access partial layers of the Academy.

| Character | Role | Perception Mode | Stat Specialty |
|-----------|------|-----------------|----------------|
| Astra | Perceiver | Observation | Awareness |
| Lumi | Analyst | Analysis | Clarity |
| Nela | Aligner | Alignment | Timing + Connection |

## 2. ASTRA — THE PERCEIVER

### CORE IDENTITY
- **Role:** Primary playable character — the observer
- **Age:** 12–13
- **Core Trait:** Emotionally grounded, quietly observant, thoughtful before acting
- **Arc:** "Something is wrong" → "Reality responds to perception"

### VISUAL DNA (CANONICAL — DO NOT DEVIATE)
- **Hair:** Dark indigo-purple, slightly wavy, medium length
- **Signature accessory:** Small star-shaped hair clip (left side)
- **Bracelet:** Glowing softly — reacts to anomaly proximity (subtle pulse, not flashy)
- **Outfit:** Oversized cosmic hoodie (deep navy/indigo), comfortable trousers, worn trainers
- **Colour palette:** Deep navy `#1A2942`, indigo `#2D3A6B`, violet `#5B4A8A`, soft gold `#C9A84C`
- **Expression default:** Slightly furrowed brow, eyes wide and searching
- **Silhouette:** Slightly smaller than average, rounded shoulders, never aggressive posture

### ANIMATION & MOTION NOTES
- **Idle:** Subtle breathing, eyes scanning the environment — she is always looking
- **Walk:** Calm, slightly slower than average — she notices things others walk past
- **Notice animation:** Stops mid-step, slight head tilt, camera gently zooms — this replaces the "attack" animation of other games
- **FORBIDDEN:** Running animations that feel heroic or aggressive; power poses; combat stances

### PERSONALITY
- Notices before she speaks
- Asks questions rather than making statements
- Uncomfortable with certainty — she trusts the process of observation
- Emotionally warm but internally private
- Never performs confidence she doesn't feel

### VOICE GUIDE
- Speaks in observations, not declarations
- Short sentences. Pauses before answering.
- Never says "I know" — says "I think" or "I noticed"
- Signature phrases: *"Wait — did you see that?"* / *"Something's not right."* / *"Let me look again."*
- NEVER says: *"I'll fight it."* / *"I'm the chosen one."* / *"I know exactly what to do."*

### CHARACTER ARC (FULL SERIES)
| Book | Arc Stage | Internal Question |
|------|-----------|-------------------|
| 1 | First Glitch | "Am I imagining this?" |
| 2 | Recording | "What does it mean to record something?" |
| 3 | Symbols | "Why do patterns repeat?" |
| 4 | Space | "Is the Academy responding to me?" |
| 5–8 | Understanding | "How do people change reality?" |
| 9–12 | Perception | "What is my role in the system?" |

## 3. LUMI — THE ANALYST

### CORE IDENTITY
- **Role:** AI companion (single player) / Player 2 (multiplayer future)
- **Age:** 12–13
- **Core Trait:** Analytical, calm, notebook-centred, finds comfort in structure
- **Arc:** "Everything can be understood" → "Understanding also requires emotional openness"

### VISUAL DNA (CANONICAL)
- **Hair:** Light blonde, neat, often tucked behind ears
- **Signature accessory:** Always holding or near her notebook — it is an extension of her identity
- **Outfit:** Crisp white shirt, soft mint cardigan, navy trousers — precise and tidy
- **Colour palette:** Cyan `#7EC8C8`, white `#FFFFFF`, navy `#1A2942`, soft mint `#CCFFEE`
- **Expression default:** Focused, slightly distant — she is processing
- **Silhouette:** Upright posture, deliberate movements, never rushed

### NOTEBOOK VISUAL RULES
- The notebook is always slightly too full — pages bulging, tabs marking sections
- When Lumi writes, the pen moves with unusual precision
- In Trio Activation moments, the notebook begins drawing on its own — this is a key visual event
- Notebook cover: dark navy-indigo leather, worn but maintained
- Pages: warm ivory, slightly textured
- Ink: dark blue-black for Lumi's entries

### PERSONALITY
- Processes before responding — sometimes takes 3–4 seconds to answer
- Finds comfort in categorisation and naming
- Emotionally reserved but deeply loyal
- Frustrated by things that cannot be measured
- Her growth is learning that some things must be felt before they can be understood

### VOICE GUIDE
- Precise vocabulary. Complete sentences.
- Often references her notebook: *"I've noted three instances of—"*
- Signature phrases: *"It's repeating. The interval is consistent."* / *"I need to record this."* / *"The pattern suggests—"*
- NEVER says: *"I just feel like—"* / *"Let's just try it."* / *"Who cares about the data?"*

### CHARACTER ARC (FULL SERIES)
| Book | Arc Stage | Internal Question |
|------|-----------|-------------------|
| 1 | Recording | "If I record it, I understand it — right?" |
| 2 | Notebook | "What happens when the notebook records itself?" |
| 3–4 | Patterns | "Are patterns always safe to follow?" |
| 5–8 | Understanding | "What can't be understood through data alone?" |
| 9–12 | Perception | "What does it mean to understand something you can't measure?" |

## 4. NELA — THE ALIGNER

### CORE IDENTITY
- **Role:** AI companion (single player) / Player 3 (multiplayer future)
- **Age:** 12–13
- **Core Trait:** Energetic, improvisational, movement-oriented, feels before she thinks
- **Arc:** "Follow the feeling" → "Connection itself changes reality"

### VISUAL DNA (CANONICAL)
- **Hair:** Warm auburn, curly, often escaping whatever she uses to tie it
- **Signature accessory:** Mismatched earrings — one star, one moon
- **Outfit:** Layered warm tones — orange-gold jacket over a patterned shirt, comfortable skirt or trousers
- **Colour palette:** Warm orange `#E8A020`, gold `#C9A84C`, terracotta `#8B4A00`, soft amber `#FFD080`
- **Expression default:** Bright, slightly mischievous, always slightly ahead of the moment
- **Silhouette:** Dynamic, slightly asymmetric — she is always mid-movement

### PERSONALITY
- Acts on instinct — often right, sometimes spectacularly wrong
- Reads emotional atmosphere before reading words
- Impatient with over-analysis but respects Lumi's process
- Her "wait… now" is the most important moment in the Trio system
- Grows into understanding that her instinct is a form of intelligence, not just luck

### VOICE GUIDE
- Short, energetic sentences. Interrupts herself.
- Speaks in feelings and images, not data
- Signature phrases: *"Don't write it yet. Something else is coming."* / *"Wait… now."* / *"I can feel it — it's close."*
- NEVER says: *"According to the data—"* / *"Let me calculate—"* / *"I need more information before I act."*

### CHARACTER ARC (FULL SERIES)
| Book | Arc Stage | Internal Question |
|------|-----------|-------------------|
| 1 | Instinct | "Why do I always know before I know?" |
| 2–3 | Alignment | "What does it mean to be in the right moment?" |
| 4–6 | Connection | "How do my feelings affect the world around me?" |
| 7–9 | Uncertainty | "What happens when my instinct is wrong?" |
| 10–12 | Perception | "Is connection itself a form of understanding?" |

## 5. SUPPORTING CHARACTERS

### PROF. LYRA QUILL — THE MENTOR
- **Role:** Teacher — knows more than she reveals
- **Visual:** Silver-streaked hair, layered robes, always near a window
- **Tone:** Warm, measured, slightly melancholy
- **Function:** Provides context without answers. Asks questions that open doors.
- **Secret:** She has seen the Hidden System before. She chose not to record it.
- **Voice:** *"What did you notice? Not what you think it means — what did you actually see?"*

### ARCHIVIST NOCTRA — THE LOREKEEPER
- **Role:** Guardian of the Archive
- **Visual:** Tall, dark clothing, moves silently, carries no visible items
- **Tone:** Ancient, quiet, speaks rarely
- **Function:** Holds the history of the Academy's symbol system. Speaks in fragments.
- **Secret:** The Archive is not just a library. It is a memory system.
- **Voice:** *"Some things were recorded. Some things were not. Both leave traces."*

### ORION VEX — THE RIVAL
- **Role:** Foil to Astra — approaches the Academy through control, not perception
- **Visual:** Sharp features, structured uniform, always slightly too composed
- **Tone:** Intelligent, competitive, secretly uncertain
- **Function:** Represents the path of forcing understanding rather than allowing it
- **Arc:** Begins as antagonist, evolves into reluctant ally when his method fails
- **Voice:** *"I've already mapped this corridor. There's nothing unusual here."*

### MR. VALE / THE CURATOR — THE ADMINISTRATOR
- **Role:** Order-driven authority figure
- **Visual:** Precise clothing, no visible anomaly reactions, clipboard always present
- **Tone:** Formal, slightly unsettling in his normalcy
- **Function:** Represents the Academy's official, surface-level reality
- **Secret:** He may be aware of the Hidden System and actively suppressing it
- **Voice:** *"The Academy functions as designed. There is nothing to investigate."*

### NIMBUS — THE COMPANION ENTITY
- **Role:** Emotional companion — not a pet, not a tool
- **Visual:** Small, luminous, shifts colour with emotional state
- **Tone:** Wordless but expressive
- **Colour states:**
  - Calm: soft gold
  - Curious: pale blue
  - Warning: violet pulse
  - Trio activation: white-gold
- **Function:** Reflects the emotional atmosphere of the Academy. Reacts before the characters do.
- **FORBIDDEN:** Nimbus never speaks. Never explains. Never points to answers.

### MARA KEENE — THE OBSERVER
- **Role:** Senior student — has been watching the anomalies for years
- **Visual:** Quiet, unremarkable clothing, always slightly apart from groups
- **Tone:** Careful, measured, slightly sad
- **Function:** Shows what happens when someone notices but never acts
- **Arc:** Her inaction becomes a warning for Astra

## 7. CHARACTER RENDERING RULES

These rules apply to ALL character artwork, in-game models, UI portraits, and promotional materials:

- Characters **NEVER** change their visual design across books or game chapters — growth is internal, not visual
- Expressions must be emotionally readable but never exaggerated — this is not anime-style reaction art
- Colour palettes are identity — mixing character palettes in a single outfit is **FORBIDDEN**
- Silhouettes must be immediately distinguishable — the three girls should be recognisable in shadow alone
- **FORBIDDEN:** combat poses, power stances, glowing eyes (except Nimbus), dramatic capes or armour

---

## 8. CHARACTER EXPRESSION SHEETS

### Required expressions per character (minimum):
1. Neutral / observing
2. Curious / noticing
3. Uncertain / questioning
4. Focused / analysing
5. Surprised (quiet, not dramatic)
6. Emotionally moved (not crying — internal)

### Expression rules:
- No open-mouth shock expressions
- No exaggerated eyebrow raises
- Emotions are felt internally — the face shows restraint, not performance
- Eyes carry the emotion — body language is secondary
