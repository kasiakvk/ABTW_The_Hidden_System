## 12. WORLD CONTINUITY RULES

### What Cannot Change Between Chapters:
1. Character visual design — locked permanently
2. Symbol meanings — once established, never revised
3. The Academy's core nature — always a perception system
4. The Golden Rule — "The Academy does not reveal itself. It responds to perception."
5. Echo entries — once created, always present

### What Changes Between Chapters:
1. Season and time of day
2. Symbol states (can progress, never regress)
3. Player stats (always increase, never decrease)
4. World stability (can fluctuate)
5. Notebook entries (always accumulate)

### Continuity Violation Flags:
- ⚠️ **CHARACTER DRIFT:** Any visual change to Astra, Lumi, or Nela
- ⚠️ **SYMBOL REGRESSION:** A symbol returning to a lower state
- ⚠️ **KNOWLEDGE VIOLATION:** A character knowing something before they learned it
- ⚠️ **TONE DRIFT:** Any environment feeling threatening rather than mysterious
- ⚠️ **SPECTACLE VIOLATION:** Any anomaly that is flashy or dramatic
