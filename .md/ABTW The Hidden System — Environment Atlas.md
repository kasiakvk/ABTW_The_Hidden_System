## 1. ENVIRONMENT DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

Every environment in ABTW must serve three simultaneous functions:
1. **Narrative function** — what story does this space tell?
2. **Mechanical function** — what gameplay does this space support?
3. **Emotional function** — what should the player feel here?

### THE GOLDEN ENVIRONMENT RULE
> *"The Academy does not reveal itself. It responds to perception."*

No environment is static. Every space has at least two states: surface reality and a deeper layer accessible through awareness, clarity, and connection.

## 3. EAST CORRIDOR — PRIMARY ENVIRONMENT

### Identity
The East Corridor is the player's first experience of the Academy and the most important space in Chapter 1. It must establish the visual language of the entire game. Everything the player learns about how to see the world begins here.

### Architecture Specification
- **Ceiling:** High vaulted — minimum 4× character height
- **Walls:** Stone with warm wood accents — aged but maintained
- **Windows:** Large, on the right side only — warm afternoon light enters at a low angle
- **Floor:** Dark polished stone — reflects light imperfectly (reflections are slightly wrong even in normal state)
- **Scale rule:** Corridors must feel larger than the characters — this is a design rule, not a suggestion
- **Texture:** Aged but maintained — not crumbling, not pristine; lived-in and ancient

### Lighting Profile
| Source | Temperature | Direction | Purpose |
|--------|-------------|-----------|---------|
| Window light | 3200K (warm gold) | Low angle, right side | Reality anchor |
| Ceiling ambient | 5500K (cool) | Diffused downward | Cognitive depth |
| Combined effect | Mixed | Creates depth shadows | Anomaly visibility |

### Three States — Visual Guide

**NORMAL STATE**
- Shadows behave correctly
- Reflections are accurate
- Light is warm and stable
- Sound: ambient hum, distant footsteps
- Emotional tone: calm, slightly too perfect

**GLITCH STATE**
- Shadows delayed 0.3–0.5 seconds
- Reflections show slightly different content
- Light flickers once, then stabilises
- Sound: low hum, slight echo distortion
- Emotional tone: productive unease, not fear

**ACTIVATION STATE**
- Symbols begin to form on surfaces
- Light becomes directional — pointing toward the pattern
- Gold intensifies, violet undertone appears
- Sound: silence, then soft pulse
- Emotional tone: wonder, alignment, discovery

### Key Props
- Stone benches along the walls (students sit here between classes)
- Notice boards with handwritten announcements
- A single window seat — Astra's preferred observation point
- Worn stone floor showing centuries of footsteps

### Anomaly Locations
- **SYM_001 (Shadow Delay):** Near the third window from the entrance
- **SYM_008 (Echo Corridor):** At the far end, near the junction

## 4. WEST CORRIDOR — MEMORY SPACE

### Identity
The West Corridor is the acoustic mirror of the East Corridor. Where the East Corridor is about visual perception, the West Corridor is about auditory memory. Sound here carries traces of the past.

### Architecture Specification
- **Ceiling:** Slightly lower than East Corridor — creates acoustic intimacy
- **Walls:** Darker stone, fewer windows — cooler light
- **Floor:** Same dark polished stone, but older — more worn
- **Acoustic quality:** Slightly reverberant — sounds linger longer than they should

### Lighting Profile
- Cooler overall — less warm gold, more ambient blue
- Shadows are slightly deeper
- No direct window light — only reflected and ambient

### Anomaly Locations
- **SYM_008 (Echo Corridor):** The primary anomaly — footstep echoes with wrong rhythm

---

## 5. OBSERVATORY — WONDER SPACE

### Identity
The Observatory is the Academy's emotional peak in Chapter 1. It is where the player first understands that the Academy's anomalies are not random — they are a system. The scale here is cosmic.

### Architecture Specification
- **Dome:** High circular ceiling — the largest interior space in the Academy
- **Telescope:** Central prop — ancient, brass, slightly too large for the room
- **Floor:** Circular, with an incomplete circle symbol permanently etched — the gap rotates between visits
- **Walls:** Dark navy stone — the colour of deep space
- **Windows:** None — the only light comes from the dome opening and the star map above

### Lighting Profile
| Source | Temperature | Quality | Purpose |
|--------|-------------|---------|---------|
| Star map glow | Cool blue-white | Diffused | Cosmic atmosphere |
| Telescope lamp | Warm gold | Focused | Observational anchor |
| Ambient | Deep navy | Very low | Depth and mystery |

### Atmosphere Specification
- Cool navy atmosphere overall
- Gold observational light from the telescope
- Layered silence — sound is absorbed, not reflected
- Emotional stillness — the space demands quiet
- Distant stars visible through the dome opening

### Key Props
- The brass telescope (central, unmovable)
- Star charts on the walls — none match known constellations
- A single chair — worn, positioned for long observation sessions
- The incomplete circle on the floor — always missing one segment

### Anomaly Locations
- **SYM_002 (Incomplete Circle):** The floor symbol — always present, always incomplete
- **SYM_007 (Observatory Star Map):** The ceiling — only visible to Trio

## 6. ARCHIVE / LIBRARY — KNOWLEDGE SPACE

### Identity
The Archive is the Academy's memory — a space where past events leave visible traces and knowledge is always incomplete. The deeper you go, the older and stranger it becomes.

### Architecture Specification
- **Structure:** Underground levels — the Archive extends downward, not upward
- **Shelves:** Floor-to-ceiling, narrow passages between stacks
- **Lighting gradient:** Warm amber at Level 1 → cool blue at Level 3
- **Books:** Some titles are readable, some are in unknown scripts, some change title between visits
- **Mirrors:** Placed throughout — reflections show different words than the originals they reflect

### Level System

**Level 1 — Surface Archive**
- Warm amber lighting
- Readable books, normal behaviour
- Students study here regularly
- Anomaly frequency: Low

**Level 2 — Deep Archive**
- Mixed warm/cool lighting
- Some books in unknown scripts
- Fewer students, quieter
- Anomaly frequency: Medium

**Level 3 — Hidden Archive**
- Cool blue lighting only
- Books that change content between readings
- No students — only Archivist Noctra
- Anomaly frequency: High

### Key Props
- Reading lamps (warm amber) at regular intervals
- Archivist Noctra's desk — always slightly too organised
- A catalogue system that doesn't quite make sense
- Mirrors at the end of each aisle

### Anomaly Locations
- **SYM_003 (Reflected Word):** Mirror at the end of the main aisle, Level 1

## 7. CLASSROOMS — SOCIAL SPACE

### Identity
Classrooms are the Academy's most "normal" spaces — deliberately so. They provide contrast to the anomaly-rich corridors and create the social warmth that makes the Academy feel inhabited.

### Architecture Specification
- **Layout:** Traditional — rows of desks facing a teaching wall
- **Windows:** Large, warm light — the most normally lit spaces in the Academy
- **Ceiling:** Lower than corridors — intimate, focused
- **Walls:** Warm stone with educational materials — maps, diagrams, notes

### Emotional Function
- Grounding — after anomaly encounters, classrooms feel safe
- Social — students interact naturally here
- Contrast — the normalcy makes anomalies elsewhere more striking

### Anomaly Frequency: Very Low
Classrooms are intentionally anomaly-free in Chapter 1. This is a design choice — the contrast between classroom normalcy and corridor strangeness is part of the perceptual system.

## 8. DORMITORIES — SAFETY SPACE

### Identity
Dormitories are the Academy's most personal spaces — where characters are most vulnerable and most themselves. They represent belonging, memory, and emotional safety.

### Architecture Specification
- **Astra's room:** Small, personal, slightly cluttered with observations
- **Shared elements:** Warm lighting, soft textures, personal objects
- **Windows:** Smaller than corridors — intimate, not expansive
- **Ceiling:** Low — creates a sense of shelter

### Astra's Room — Specific Details
- A desk covered in observation notes
- A window that looks toward the East Corridor
- The star-shaped hair clip on the desk when not worn
- A notebook (not Lumi's — Astra's own, less organised)
- Warm amber lamp — always on

### Anomaly Locations
- **SYM_005 (Memory Trace):** Dormitory Hall — visible only at night

### Emotional Function
- Recovery — players return here after intense anomaly sequences
- Memory — the room holds traces of Astra's observations
- Safety — the one space where the Academy feels completely benign

## 9. ACADEMY GARDENS — REFLECTION SPACE

### Identity
The Gardens are the Academy's breathing space — where nature and architecture integrate. They are calming, symbolically layered, and emotionally restorative.

### Architecture Specification
- **Layout:** Formal garden structure with hidden informal areas
- **Plants:** Unusual varieties — some grow in patterns that mirror the symbol system
- **Pathways:** Stone, worn smooth, leading to hidden areas
- **Water features:** Small, reflective — mirrors for the sky

### Seasonal Variations

| Season | Visual | Anomaly Behaviour | Emotional Tone |
|--------|--------|-------------------|----------------|
| Autumn | Warm gold and amber, falling leaves | Memory traces visible | Melancholy, warmth |
| Winter | White, still, crystalline | Stability +10%, fewer anomalies | Clarity, isolation |
| Spring | Fresh green, new growth | New symbols emerge | Renewal, curiosity |
| Summer | Full green, warm light | Anomalies most social | Warmth, openness |

### Anomaly Locations
- **SYM_006 (Garden Spiral):** Central garden — the mathematically precise plant spiral

## 10. ROOFTOPS — PERSPECTIVE SPACE

### Identity
Rooftops are the Academy's revelatory spaces — where the player can see the whole picture for the first time. They are used for climactic moments and major revelations.

### Architecture Specification
- **Access:** Hidden staircases, not obvious
- **Surface:** Stone, slightly worn — students have been here before
- **View:** The entire Academy grounds visible from here
- **Sky:** Always more dramatic than ground level — stars brighter, clouds more present

### Emotional Function
- Perspective — seeing the Academy as a whole system
- Revelation — major story beats happen here
- Solitude — a space for processing, not socialising

### Anomaly Frequency: High
Rooftops have the highest anomaly frequency outside of Hidden Halls. The elevated perspective allows the player to see patterns that are invisible at ground level.

---

## 11. HIDDEN HALLS — DEEP MYSTERY SPACE

### Identity
Hidden Halls are the Academy's deepest spaces — accessible only through Trio activation and maximum perception. They represent the Hidden System itself.

### Architecture Specification
- **Access:** Only through Trio activation — no physical door
- **Appearance:** Similar to normal corridors but slightly wrong in every detail
- **Lighting:** Soft gold — the activation colour
- **Scale:** Slightly larger than normal spaces — the proportions are off

### Emotional Function
- Discovery — the deepest layer of the Academy
- Completion — the reward for patience and observation
- Mystery — even here, the system doesn't fully explain itself

### Anomaly Frequency: Very High
Hidden Halls are saturated with Layer 3 symbols. Every surface holds information.

## 13. ENVIRONMENT PRODUCTION RULES

### For Graphic Artists:
1. Every environment must feel **inhabited** — signs of student life everywhere
2. Scale: environments must feel **larger than the characters**
3. Lighting must serve the **emotional function** of the space
4. Anomalies must be **subtle** — 70% normal, 30% strange
5. **FORBIDDEN:** empty showcase environments, game-level feeling, fantasy theme park aesthetics

### Environment QA Checklist:
- [ ] Does the space feel emotionally correct for its function?
- [ ] Are there signs of student life and habitation?
- [ ] Does the scale feel larger than the characters?
- [ ] Is the lighting emotionally appropriate?
- [ ] Are anomalies subtle enough (70/30 rule)?
- [ ] Does the space feel like a real place, not a game level?
